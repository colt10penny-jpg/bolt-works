Engineer Empire: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Engineer Empire is an idle factory game for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. In short: there are no accounts, we never ask for your name, email or contacts, and your game is saved on your own device. The game uses anonymous play statistics to help us improve it, and shows ads from Google AdMob.

1. Your saved game

Your towns, money, levels and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Choosing Settings > Start over in the game, or uninstalling the app, deletes them.

2. Anonymous play statistics

To see which parts of the game people enjoy and where they get stuck, the game sends a small number of events to PostHog, an analytics service, on servers in the United States. Each event contains:

  • a random player ID the game makes when you first open it (it is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any other account);
  • what happened in the game, for example opening the app, building a factory, finishing an order, launching a rocket or watching an ad, and progress such as how many rockets you have launched;
  • which day of play it is, the app version, and whether you are on iPhone, Android or the web.

We do not send your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any advertising identifier to PostHog. Like any internet service, PostHog receives your device's IP address when the game connects to it; we do not use it to identify you. You can find your player ID in Settings in the game if you want us to delete the statistics linked to it (see section 7).

3. Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional ads you can choose to watch for rewards. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (and an approximate location based on it), device type and settings, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • On iPhone, the game asks whether it may track you before any advertising identifier is used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised using that identifier. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only learn that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it.

5. What we don't do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not ask for, or have access to, your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • The game has no accounts, chat or ways to share personal information with other players.

6. Children

Engineer Empire is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can delete everything saved on your device with Start over or by uninstalling the game. You can ask us to see or delete the anonymous statistics linked to your player ID by emailing us with that ID. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how it is used; contact us and we will help. We keep anonymous statistics only for as long as they are useful for improving the game.

8. Security

Information sent by the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, but we keep what we collect to a minimum.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy: use the support contact on the game's App Store or Google Play page.
